Debt Payoff Strategies: Snowball vs Avalanche

Debt Snowball (Smallest Balance First)

Pay minimums on all debts, throw extra money at the smallest balance first. When it's paid off, roll that payment to the next smallest.

Pros: Quick wins build motivation

Cons: May pay more total interest

Debt Avalanche (Highest Interest First)

Pay minimums on all debts, throw extra money at the highest interest rate first. When it's paid off, roll that payment to the next highest.

Pros: Saves the most money on interest

Cons: Can feel slow at first
