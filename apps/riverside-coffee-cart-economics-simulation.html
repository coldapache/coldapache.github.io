The Riverside Coffee Cart

You just took over a coffee cart in the town square. Five days, one tight budget, and a rival across the way. Every choice has a cost. Can you turn this week around?

Your Story

You are the new owner of the Riverside Coffee Cart

The keys land in your hand on a cold Monday morning. The cart is yours now: a battered counter, a worn awning, and a square full of people who have never heard of you. Across the cobblestones, a rival cart is already steaming. You have five days to win this corner.

There is never enough money, time, or supplies to do everything you want. That is the heart of economics: making the best choices when resources are scarce. Each day this week you will face a real decision, spend what little you have, and watch the trade-offs ripple through your cash, your customers, and your reputation. The week builds to one big day: the town festival.

The four ideas you will live this week

⚖️
ScarcityYour money, time, and supplies are limited, so you can never choose everything at once.
🔄
Trade-offsChoosing one option means giving up the others. Every decision is a trade.
🎯
Opportunity CostThe value of the next-best option you gave up when you made your choice.
💰
Cost and BenefitSmart owners weigh what an option costs against what it gives back.

How each day plays out

  1. Read the morning's situation and check your scarce resources.
  2. Pick one option. You can only choose one, just like in real life.
  3. Watch the square react: your cash, customers, and reputation move in real time.
  4. See the opportunity cost of what you gave up, then open the cart for the next day.
