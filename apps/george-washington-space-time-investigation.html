1
Move through time. Use the timeline at the bottom (or the arrow keys, or Play). Every stop is a real decision or turning point in this life. Every pin on the map is clickable at any time; hollow pins are moments you have not visited yet.
2
Watch the map and the web react. Each event sends dashed arcs to the places it changed, and the portrait web lights up whoever was pulled into that moment.
3
Build the case files. Every event you visit logs evidence. Finish an era, then open its case file in the header and deliver your verdict. Crack all five to unlock the Historian's Verdict.
